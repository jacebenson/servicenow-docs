ServiceNow sells a lot of products. This section maps what they are, roughly how they relate, and what you'd use them for.

This is based on my memory and what's publicly available on the [entitlements page](https://www.servicenow.com/products/entitlements.html).

## Now Platform

The foundation everything runs on. App Engine, IntegrationHub, Flow Designer, and the underlying platform capabilities — tables, ACLs, UI policies, the works.

| Product | What it is |
| ------- | ---------- |
| **App Engine** | Build custom applications on the platform. Studio, scoped apps, App Engine Studio. |
| **IntegrationHub** | Connect ServiceNow to external systems. Spokes, REST, SOAP, custom integrations. |
| **Automation Engine** | Flow Designer and Action Designer — low-code process automation. |

## IT Service Management (ITSM)

The classic. Incident, Problem, Change, Service Catalog, Knowledge. This is where most people start.

## IT Operations Management (ITOM)

Discovery, Service Mapping, CI visibility. You know where your stuff is, how it's connected, and what's broken.

## Security Operations (SecOps)

Security Incident Response, Vulnerability Response, Threat Intelligence. Connects SIEM data to the incident workflow.

## Customer Service Management (CSM)

ITSM for non-IT teams. Customer case management, portals, omnichannel.

## HR Service Delivery (HRSD)

Employee case management — onboarding, offboarding, HR cases, knowledge, Employee Center.

## Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)

Policy and compliance management, risk assessment, vendor risk, audit management.

## IT Business Management (ITBM)

Project portfolio management, demand management, resource management, agile development (now part of Strategic Portfolio Management).

## Strategic Portfolio Management (SPM)

The evolution of ITBM. Work management across the org — projects, portfolios, demand, roadmaps.

## Field Service Management (FSM)

Dispatch, scheduling, mobile workforce management.

## Creator Workflows (now part of Platform)

App Engine, Flow Designer, Virtual Agent. The low-code/no-code tooling layer.
