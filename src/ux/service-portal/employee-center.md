Employee Center is the employee-facing portal built on Service Portal. It gives employees a single place to find HR information, submit requests, browse knowledge articles, and track their open items — all without logging into the backend.

It's not just HR. Employee Center can surface IT catalog items, facility requests, and any other service your organization offers. The idea is one front door for everything an employee might need.

## Key Features

- **Home page** — Personalized dashboard with announcements, quick links, and open request summaries
- **Knowledge base** — HR policies, IT guides, facility info — searchable and categorized
- **Service Catalog** — Request time off, order equipment, report an issue — all through catalog items
- **My Requests** — Track the status of everything you've submitted
- **Journeys** — Guided experiences for life events (onboarding, role change, leave)

## Employee Center vs. Employee Center Pro

There are two versions:

| Feature | Employee Center | Employee Center Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Portal | Single portal | Multi-portal, role-based |
| Knowledge | Basic KB | Enhanced search, AI recommendations |
| Journeys | Basic | Advanced, with Journey Designer |
| Integrations | Limited | Viva Connections, Zoom, Teams |
| Case deflection | Basic | AI-powered |

Employee Center is included with HR Service Delivery. Employee Center Pro adds the advanced features and is a separate licensing SKU.

## Configuration

Employee Center is configured through Service Portal — you customize widgets, pages, and themes like any other portal. Specific Employee Center configurations include:

- **Home page widgets** — Announcements, quick links, personalized content
- **Catalog assignment** — Which catalog items appear in Employee Center
- **Knowledge base mapping** — Which KBs are searchable
- **Header and footer** — Branding, navigation menu, global search
